Figure X. Raman spectra of 20 nm polystyrene (PS) nanoparticles. Top: 1 mg/mL PS on CaF2 (reference).
Below: SERS spectra of PS on AgSiNW at 1 mg/mL (2.3 x 10^14 NP/mL; 1 matching spectrum), 100 ug/mL
(2.3 x 10^13 NP/mL; mean of 15) and 10 ug/mL (2.3 x 10^12 NP/mL; mean of 13). Spectra were selected from
25-point maps by matching the 1001 cm-1 ring-breathing band (position, width, significance) and the
985-1045 cm-1 band shape to the CaF2 reference. Bands at ~1002, ~1600, ~2930 and ~3060 cm-1 identify PS;
519 cm-1 is the Si phonon of the nanowire substrate. 532 nm excitation, 1800 l/mm grating, 50x LWD objective;
spectra offset vertically for clarity. Particle numbers assume 20 nm spheres with PS density 1.05 g/cm3.
