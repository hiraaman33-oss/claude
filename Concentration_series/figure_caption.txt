Figure X. Raman spectra of 20 nm polystyrene (PS) nanoparticles. Top: 1 mg/mL PS drop-cast on CaF2 (reference).
Below: mean SERS spectra of PS on AgSiNW (3 uL drop each) at 1 mg/mL (2.3 x 10^14 NP/mL), 100 ug/mL
(2.3 x 10^13 NP/mL) and 10 ug/mL (2.3 x 10^12 NP/mL). Each spectrum is the mean of the 4 map spectra (of 25)
that best match the CaF2 reference, ranked by the significance and shape of the 1001 cm-1 ring-breathing band.
Bands at ~1002 (1), ~1190 (2), ~1600 (3), ~2930 (4) and ~3060 (5) cm-1 were used to identify PS; numbers are
shown only where the band is resolved from the substrate background. The band at 519 cm-1 is the Si phonon of
the nanowire substrate. Spectra were acquired at 532 nm with an 1800 l/mm grating and 50x LWD objective
(1 s x 2 accumulations; 1 mg/mL map 1 s x 4); baseline-corrected (asymmetric least squares).
Particle numbers assume 20 nm spheres with PS density 1.05 g/cm3.
