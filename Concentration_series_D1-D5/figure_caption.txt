Figure X. Raman spectra of 20 nm polystyrene (PS) nanoparticles. Top: 1 mg/mL PS on CaF2 (reference).
Below: SERS spectra of PS on AgSiNW at D1 = 1 mg/mL (2.3 x 10^14 NP/mL; mean of 11 of 25 map spectra),
D2 = 100 ug/mL (2.3 x 10^13 NP/mL; mean of 15/25), D3 = 10 ug/mL (2.3 x 10^12 NP/mL; mean of 13/25),
D4 = 1 ug/mL (2.3 x 10^11 NP/mL; mean of 2/25) and D5 = 0.5 ug/mL (1.1 x 10^11 NP/mL; mean of 7/20).
Spectra were selected from each map by matching the 1001 cm-1 ring-breathing band (position, width,
significance) and the 985-1045 cm-1 band shape to the CaF2 reference, after cosmic-ray removal.
PS bands: ~1002 (1), ~1190 (2), ~1600 (3), ~2930 (4), ~3060 (5) cm-1; numbers are shown only where the band
is resolved. 519 cm-1 is the Si phonon of the nanowire substrate. 532 nm excitation, 1800 l/mm grating,
50x LWD objective; spectra offset vertically for clarity. Particle numbers assume 20 nm spheres, 1.05 g/cm3.
