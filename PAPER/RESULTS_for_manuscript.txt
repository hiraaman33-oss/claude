--- LIMITATIONS TO STATE ---

* If all EV spectra come from one preparation and all NV spectra from another, preparation or measurement-session effects cannot be separated from EV-vs-NV biology.
* After SNV, intensities are relative (composition), not absolute amounts.
* Fig 3 b/d (LDA on all spectra) shows the separation; the classification performance is the cross-validation (Fig 5, Table 1).
