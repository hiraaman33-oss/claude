RESULTS - Acacia EVs vs NVs on Ag/SiNWs (generated by Acacia_PCA_LDA_Explained.py)
==============================================================================

Data: 120 EV and 120 NV SERS spectra, 6 measurement sites per group, 20 spectra per site. Pre-processing: cosmic-ray removal, 2 cm-1 grid, Savitzky-Golay (11 pts, 3rd order), ALS baseline, SNV.

--- FINGERPRINT REGION (250-1790 CM-1) ---

1. Unsupervised PCA. PC1 and PC2 explain 16.8% and 8.9% of the variance (PC1-PC5: 41.0%). The groups separate mainly along PC1 (mean PC1 score: EVs +4.76, NVs -4.76), although the labels were not used.
   PC1 loading, main bands (cm-1; relatively stronger in): 1033 (NVs), 1159 (EVs), 1353 (EVs), 1389 (NVs), 1499 (EVs), 1587 (EVs), 1609 (NVs), 1629 (NVs).

2. Difference spectrum NV - EV (site means, +/- 2 SE): the difference exceeds 2 SE at 65% of the wavenumbers. Largest consistent differences: 1033 (NVs higher), 1161 (EVs higher), 1355 (EVs higher), 1391 (NVs higher), 1497 (EVs higher), 1589 (EVs higher), 1609 (NVs higher), 1629 (NVs higher).
   (Relative intensities after SNV normalisation, not absolute amounts.)

3. PCA-LDA (5 PCs), displayed on all spectra: median canonical variable 1 -2.06 (EVs) vs +2.05 (NVs); Welch t-test on the 6 + 6 site means p = 3.9e-08. Descriptive only (LDA fitted on the same spectra).

4. Classification of new sites (5-fold cross-validation, whole sites left out):
   PCA-LDA: accuracy 95.4% (229/240 spectra), sensitivity (NV) 95.0%, specificity (EV) 95.8%; EVs 115/120, NVs 114/120 correct.
   PLS-DA: accuracy 95.4% (229/240 spectra), sensitivity (NV) 95.0%, specificity (EV) 95.8%; EVs 115/120, NVs 114/120 correct.
   Stricter check (PCA re-fitted inside each training fold): PCA-LDA 96.2%.

5. Exact permutation test (all 924 assignments of 6 of the 12 sites to "EV"):
   PCA-LDA: shuffled labels give a median accuracy of 50.4% (95th percentile 78.3%); p = 0.0022.
   PLS-DA: shuffled labels give a median accuracy of 49.6% (95th percentile 69.2%); p = 0.0022.

--- CH-STRETCHING REGION (2760-3095 CM-1) ---

1. Unsupervised PCA. PC1 and PC2 explain 33.3% and 14.0% of the variance (PC1-PC5: 66.9%). The groups separate mainly along PC1 (mean PC1 score: EVs -1.88, NVs +1.88), although the labels were not used.
   PC1 loading, main bands (cm-1; relatively stronger in): 2887 (NVs), 2925 (EVs), 2963 (NVs), 3063 (EVs).

2. Difference spectrum NV - EV (site means, +/- 2 SE): the difference exceeds 2 SE at 79% of the wavenumbers. Largest consistent differences: 2885 (NVs higher), 2925 (EVs higher), 2957 (NVs higher), 3067 (EVs higher).
   (Relative intensities after SNV normalisation, not absolute amounts.)

3. PCA-LDA (5 PCs), displayed on all spectra: median canonical variable 1 -2.49 (EVs) vs +2.64 (NVs); Welch t-test on the 6 + 6 site means p = 2.3e-11. Descriptive only (LDA fitted on the same spectra).

4. Classification of new sites (5-fold cross-validation, whole sites left out):
   PCA-LDA: accuracy 97.1% (233/240 spectra), sensitivity (NV) 99.2%, specificity (EV) 95.0%; EVs 114/120, NVs 119/120 correct.
   PLS-DA: accuracy 98.8% (237/240 spectra), sensitivity (NV) 99.2%, specificity (EV) 98.3%; EVs 118/120, NVs 119/120 correct.
   Stricter check (PCA re-fitted inside each training fold): PCA-LDA 96.7%.

5. Exact permutation test (all 924 assignments of 6 of the 12 sites to "EV"):
   PCA-LDA: shuffled labels give a median accuracy of 53.5% (95th percentile 80.4%); p = 0.0022.
   PLS-DA: shuffled labels give a median accuracy of 49.2% (95th percentile 75.0%); p = 0.0022.

--- WHAT THESE RESULTS DO AND DO NOT SHOW ---
* The EV and NV spectra differ reproducibly: a model trained on some sites classifies the spectra of sites it has never seen with the accuracies above, far above the accuracies obtained with shuffled labels.
* The independent unit is the measurement site (6 per group), not the spectrum (120 per group). With 6 + 6 sites the smallest possible permutation p is 2/924 = 0.0022 (the real labeling and its EV<->NV mirror image give the same accuracy); p = 0.0022 means that no other way of splitting the 12 sites reaches the real accuracy.
* If all EV spectra come from one preparation and all NV spectra from another, the comparison cannot separate EV-vs-NV biology from preparation or measurement-session effects; independent replicate preparations would address this.
* After SNV, band intensities are relative (composition), not absolute amounts.
