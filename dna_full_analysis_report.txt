     The 5–15 cm-1 scatter reflects hot-spot-to-hotspot geometry variation.

  4. THE 2930/2977 cm-1 PEAKS ARE HaCaT CELL CH STRETCHES.
     These are NOT in S1 (S1 only goes to 1800 cm-1).
     They are real SERS-enhanced lipid/protein CH modes from HaCaT cells,
     worth reporting as evidence that the SERS substrate detects whole cells.

  5. THE DOMINANT SERS PEAKS ARE AT 1118 AND 1315 cm-1.
     In S1 the dominant peaks are at 1483 and 1575 cm-1.
     This redistribution of intensity shows SERS selectively enhances
     the phosphate backbone (1118 cm-1) and A/T/G in-plane modes (1315 cm-1)
     over the C=N nucleobase stretches. This means DNA adsorbs with its
     backbone/ribose CLOSER to the Ag surface than the nucleobases.
